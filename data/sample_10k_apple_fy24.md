## PART I

### Item 1. Business Overview & Global Product Operations
Apple Inc. ("Apple" or the "Company") designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, and accessories, and sells a variety of related services. 

The Company’s fiscal year is the 52- or 53-week period that ends on the last Saturday of September. Fiscal year 2024 ended on September 28, 2024 ("2024") and included 52 weeks. Fiscal year 2023 ended on September 30, 2023 ("2023") and included 52 weeks. Fiscal year 2022 ended on September 24, 2022 ("2022") and included 52 weeks.

The Company manages its business primarily on a geographic basis. The Company’s reportable segments consist of the Americas, Europe, Greater China, Japan, and Rest of Asia Pacific.

## PART II

### Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations (MD&A)

#### Fiscal 2024 Financial Highlights
* **Total Net Sales:** Total net sales increased 2% or $7.8 billion during 2024 compared to 2023, driven primarily by higher net sales of Services and Mac, partially offset by lower net sales of Wearables, Home and Accessories.
* **Services Net Sales:** Services reached an all-time revenue record of $96.2 billion in 2024, representing year-over-year growth of 12.9% compared to $85.2 billion in 2023.
* **Gross Margin:** Gross margin was $180.7 billion in 2024 compared to $169.1 billion in 2023, with gross margin percentage expanding to 46.2% from 44.1%.
* **Operating Expenses:** Total operating expenses were $57.5 billion in 2024 compared to $54.8 billion in 2023, reflecting continued investment in Research and Development ("R&D"), particularly in machine learning, artificial intelligence, and proprietary silicon.

### Research and Development (R&D) and Capital Expenditures

The Company believes continued investment in R&D is critical to its business and competitive position. 

* R&D expense was **$31,370 million** in 2024, compared to **$29,915 million** in 2023 and **$26,251 million** in 2022.
* R&D growth was primarily driven by headcount-related expenses and engineering development costs focused on Apple Intelligence, on-device foundation models, and custom Apple Silicon (M4 and A18 Pro architectures).
* Capital expenditures for property, plant, and equipment totaled **$9,450 million** in 2024, focusing on private cloud computing infrastructure and green data centers.

### Item 1A. Risk Factors & Regulatory Scrutiny
* **Antitrust and Regulatory Inquiries:** The Company is subject to complex regulatory investigations globally, including the European Union Digital Markets Act (DMA) compliance requirements and United States Department of Justice (DOJ) civil antitrust lawsuits alleging monopolization of smartphone markets.
* **Concentration of Manufacturing & Supply Chain:** Substantially all of the Company's hardware manufacturing is outsourced to partners in Asia, primarily mainland China, India, and Vietnam. Any disruption resulting from geopolitical tensions, natural disasters, or labor disputes could materially impact supply availability.
* **Foreign Currency Volatility:** Over 60% of the Company's net sales originate outside the United States. Fluctuations in the US Dollar relative to the Euro, Chinese Renminbi, and Japanese Yen create translation headwinds affecting reported gross margins.
